Weight Sensitivity Analysis

This report evaluates different (alpha, beta) combinations used in the combined health score:

Combined Score=α⋅Risk Score+β⋅Anomaly Score
Objective

To determine the optimal balance between:

Risk Score (rule-based stability signals)
Anomaly Score (adaptive behavior detection)

The goal is to:

Keep normal conditions stable and low
Ensure clear separation for abnormal scenarios
Methodology
Same simulated timelines used for all weight combinations
Tested across five scenarios:
Normal
CPU Stress
Memory Leak
Sudden Spikes
Critical Instability

Each combination was evaluated using:

Final score behavior
Stability (volatility)
Trend progression (upward ratio)
Overall research score

Why this works best
Highest research score (24.86)
Keeps Normal low (18.91) → avoids false alarms
Strong separation → Critical reaches 52.27

Interpretation
Higher alpha → more trust in rule-based risk
Higher beta → more trust in anomaly detection
Key Pattern Observed
Increasing beta:
Raises normal scores (bad)
Reduces separation from critical (bad)
Increasing alpha:
Keeps normal stable (good)
Improves distinction between scenarios (good)
